search terms

starting points, nothing more. once the pool has a few hundred videos the related-video trail does most of the work. untick a whole category, cross out a term, or add your own.

learning

off by default, so what you watch never changes what you're shown, like before the algorithm. switch it on and the extension counts words from titles you watch; a word only counts once it shows up in two different videos. it all stays on this computer.

resetting

"clean out" removes entries with a broken id, no title or a year past the cutoff. run it after an update. "clear out" is for after you lower the cutoff. "empty" starts over from nothing.

privacy

  • no account, no analytics, no tracking, no server of ours. nothing is ever sent to optima machina.
  • everything the extension learns and collects (the pool, your settings, your hidden channels) stays in this browser.
  • the searches it makes on its own go to youtube anonymously, without your cookies or sign-in, so they never show up in your history or change your recommendations.
  • only "save to watch later" and "save to playlist", which you click yourself, go to youtube as you.
  • the ai feature is off unless you switch it on, needs your own key, and sends only the words listed above to the model you picked. the extension can't reach those servers at all until you allow it.
  • youtube's thumbnails load from youtube's image servers, the same as on youtube.
